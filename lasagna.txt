How to Make Lasagna

To make lasagna, start by cooking ground beef with onions and garlic in a pan. Add tomato sauce, herbs like basil and oregano, and let it simmer. In a separate bowl, mix ricotta cheese with an egg and some grated parmesan.

Boil lasagna noodles until they’re soft. In a baking dish, layer the ingredients: start with a little sauce, then noodles, then the cheese mixture, and repeat. Finish with a layer of noodles, sauce, and lots of shredded mozzarella on top.

Bake the lasagna in the oven at 180°C (350°F) for about 40 minutes. Let it cool for a few minutes before serving. Enjoy your delicious homemade lasagna!
